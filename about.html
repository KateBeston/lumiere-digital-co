About

Great businesses deserve to be seen.

Lumière exists to give small businesses the kind of online presence that used to be reserved for big brands, without the big-brand price or the hassle.

The name

lumière

noun · French light

Light is what makes things visible. Without it, even the best work goes unseen. The name also nods to the Lumière brothers, who pioneered cinema in 1895 and showed the world what a single beam of light could do.

Why we exist

Too many great businesses stay in the shadows.

Every suburb is full of brilliant small businesses: the groomer with a waitlist, the café locals swear by, the tradie who always turns up. Yet many of them are almost invisible online, relying on word of mouth, a social media page or a website that hasn't been touched in years.

Meanwhile, customers are searching, comparing and booking online, often late at night, and they choose the business they can find and book easily.

Lumière closes that gap. We give small businesses a professional website and online booking that work around the clock, so owners can spend less time on admin and more time doing what they love.
