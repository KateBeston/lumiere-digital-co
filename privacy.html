Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Lumière Digital Co is a business name of Aurella Group Pty Ltd (ABN 70 649 742 423) ("we", "us", "our"). We respect your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and disclose personal information through our website, lumieredigitalco.com, and in the course of our business. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

1. What we collect

We only collect personal information that we need to respond to you and provide our services. This may include:

  • your name, business name, email address and phone number
  • details you share about your business and project
  • information needed to deliver your project, such as content, images and account access you provide
  • billing details needed to send invoices (we don't store card details; payments are processed by our payment provider)

2. How we collect it

We collect personal information directly from you, when you fill in our contact form, email or call us, or work with us on a project. We don't buy personal information from third parties.

3. How we use it

We use your personal information to:

  • respond to your enquiry and provide quotes
  • deliver, support and maintain your website and related services
  • send invoices and manage our business records
  • meet our legal obligations

We won't send you marketing emails unless you've agreed to receive them, and you can opt out at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business, and only as needed. These include:

  • our website hosting provider
  • our database provider (Supabase), which securely stores contact form submissions
  • our email, accounting and payment providers
  • professional advisers, such as our accountant, where necessary

We may also disclose information where required or authorised by law.

5. Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers store data on servers outside Australia, including in the United States. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure those providers protect your information in a way that is consistent with Australian privacy law.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use your browser's temporary session storage only to remember that you've already seen our opening animation, so it doesn't replay on every page. This information stays in your browser and is cleared when you close it.

Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts and animation code from cdnjs (Cloudflare). When your browser requests these files, those providers receive standard technical information such as your IP address. Please see their privacy policies for details.

If we introduce analytics in the future, we'll update this policy first and favour privacy-friendly tools that don't use tracking cookies.

7. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, change or disclosure. This includes using reputable providers, secure passwords and two-factor authentication where available. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

8. Client website data

When we build or maintain a website for a client, that client is responsible for the personal information collected through their own website and booking systems. We access it only as needed to provide our services.

9. Accessing or correcting your information

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing us. We'll respond within a reasonable time, usually within 30 days.

10. Links to other websites

Our website may link to other websites. We're not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

11. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we've handled your personal information, please contact us first and we'll do our best to resolve it. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
