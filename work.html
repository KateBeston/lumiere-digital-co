The challenge

Customers found the studio through Instagram but couldn't see services or prices, and every booking meant a back-and-forth of messages.

What we built

A seven-page site with services and prices, a coat-care guide, an Instagram-style gallery, a full FAQ, and online booking with deposits and gift cards.

The result

A professional home online that answers questions before they're asked and lets customers book any time, while the owner focuses on the grooming.
