Questions

Good to know

How long does a website take?

Most sites are live within five to seven business days once we have your photos, prices and details.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit to begin, and the balance before your site goes live.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain, hosting, booking accounts and website files are all in your name. We're added as a collaborator so we can help.

Which booking systems do you use?

Usually Square Appointments or Fresha, depending on your business. We set it up, add your services and connect it to your site.

Are there ongoing costs?

Your domain costs a small yearly fee, and booking platforms charge their own fees. Hosting is usually free for sites like these. The care plan is optional.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo if you have one, photos of your work, your services and prices, and your hours. We'll send a simple checklist.

What if I need changes later?

Our care plan covers ongoing updates. Without one, changes are quoted per job before we start.
